Research

Security across the computing stack

I study how algorithm mapping, microarchitecture, and circuit implementation choices shape both the efficiency and attack surface of machine-learning systems.

01

Hardware-aware ML security

Studying backdoors and fault attacks that emerge when neural networks meet real memories, accelerators, and execution platforms.

02

Side-channel security

Building practical attacks and defenses across devices and modalities, from shared system caches to physical power and EM traces.

03

Secure accelerator co-design

Treating security and privacy as first-class constraints alongside energy, accuracy, performance, and implementation cost.
