Area 01

Energy-efficient eNVM in-memory computing accelerator design

This work develops and analyzes energy-efficient hardware for machine learning, including in-memory computing and accelerator architectures. It considers the relationships among circuit non-idealities, energy, accuracy, performance, and security.

Area 02

Security vulnerabilities of ML accelerators

This work in hardware-aware machine-learning security includes side-channel analysis, fault-based attacks, backdoors, and secure accelerator design. It examines how vulnerabilities emerge when learning algorithms are deployed on physical systems.

TCAS-I 2026

HOTSPOT

On-chip differential temperature sensing for detecting localized power anomalies from hardware Trojans on a measured 65-nm CMOS prototype.

  • 95%peak Trojan detection accuracy
  • 6.65×higher SNR than supply-current sensing at 1.95 µW
  • 65 nmmeasured CMOS prototype
