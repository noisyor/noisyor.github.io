Research

Secure ML systems and energy-efficient IC design

My research connects machine-learning security with integrated-circuit and system design, spanning algorithms, computer architecture, and hardware implementation.

Secure ML Systems

My work in hardware-aware machine-learning security includes side-channel analysis, fault-based attacks, backdoors, and secure accelerator design. It examines how vulnerabilities emerge when learning algorithms are deployed on physical systems.

Energy-Efficient IC Design

I develop and analyze energy-efficient hardware for machine learning, including in-memory computing and accelerator architectures. My work considers the relationships among circuit non-idealities, energy, accuracy, performance, and security.
