Selected research projects

Project websites and public artifacts, grouped into two research areas.

Energy-efficient eNVM in-memory computing accelerator design

Modeling, benchmarking, and measured silicon for resistive in-memory computing, where device, array, sensing, and ADC non-idealities set the energy and accuracy of the accelerator.

SEC–MRAM IMC

Behavioral modeling, circuit design, and measured 22 nm MRAM in-memory-computing silicon with statistical error compensation.

Explore the project·View repository

Crossbar IMC analysis

Computational-accuracy limits of resistive crossbar in-memory computing across MRAM, ReRAM, and FeFET device models.

Explore the project·View repository

Parallel-bar IMC analysis

Energy-accuracy trade-offs in resistive parallel-bar in-memory computing, modeling how device, array, sensing, and ADC non-idealities jointly limit SNDR across MRAM, ReRAM, and FeFET.

Read the paper·Explore the project·View repository

IMC benchmarking

An open benchmarking resource for in-memory-computing architectures with indexed chip data, normalized metrics, and reproducible plots.

Explore the project·View repository

Security vulnerabilities of ML accelerators

Side-channel, fault-injection, and hardware Trojan threats to the hardware that runs machine learning, together with the measurements and defenses that expose them.

HOTSPOT

On-chip differential temperature sensing for detecting localized power anomalies from hardware Trojans on a measured 65-nm CMOS prototype.

Attack from Shadows
